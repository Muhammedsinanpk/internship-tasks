
The Superstore Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business performance from 2011–2014. The company generated 2.30M in total sales and 286.40K in total profit from 5K orders, serving 793 customers with a 12.47% profit margin and an average order value of 458.61.

Key insights include:

West region recorded the highest sales, followed by East, while South generated the lowest sales.
Consumer segment contributed the largest share of total sales (approximately 50.5%), making it the most valuable customer segment.
Technology category produced the highest profit, followed by Office Supplies, whereas Furniture generated the lowest profit.
Sales and profit showed an overall upward trend from 2011 to 2014, indicating consistent business growth over the years.
Interactive filters for Year, Region, Segment, Category, Sub-Category, and Ship Mode allow users to explore performance across different business dimensions.
