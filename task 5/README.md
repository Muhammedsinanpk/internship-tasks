Summary of EDA
Dataset contains 891 passengers and 12 features.
Missing values are present in Age, Cabin, and Embarked.
Most passengers belong to Third Class.
More males travelled than females.
Females had significantly higher survival rates.
First-class passengers had better survival chances.
Most passengers were aged between 20–40 years.
Fare distribution is highly skewed with several outliers.
Fare has a positive relationship with survival, while Passenger Class has a negative relationship.
No duplicate records were found.
